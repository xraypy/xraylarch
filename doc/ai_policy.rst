====================
AI Policy
====================

Contributions to Larch that use generative AI tools (such as large language
models) must follow the `Larch AI policy`_, kept in the ``AI_POLICY.md`` file
at the top of the source repository. In short: you are responsible for, and
must understand, any code you submit; you must disclose any AI assistance in
your pull request; and autonomous AI agents may not submit pull requests.

.. _Larch AI policy: https://github.com/xraypy/xraylarch/blob/master/AI_POLICY.md
